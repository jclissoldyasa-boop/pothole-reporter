      Delete all reports?

      Pothole been fixed? Tap Fixed: it comes off the map but still counts. Delete (or swipe left) is for mistakes: it also removes the pothole from the riders' map and the counts.

      One tap from your home screen

      No time to open the app when a pothole comes up? Set up a home-screen button that logs one the moment you tap it.

      Android:

      1. Add the app to your home screen: browser menu (⋮), then Add to Home screen or Install app.
      2. Press and hold the Moto Potholes icon. A Log a pothole shortcut pops up. Tap it and the pothole is logged straight away.
      3. For a button of its own, drag Log a pothole onto your home screen.

      iPhone: Share button, then Add to Home Screen. It opens with the big button at the top, ready to tap.

      Location needs to be allowed for this app, or it will ask the first time.

      Riders' pothole map

      Every pothole riders have logged. Only the spot and how long ago are shared. Tap a pin to say it's gone, “fixed”, or still there. Once enough riders say it's gone, it comes off the map.
