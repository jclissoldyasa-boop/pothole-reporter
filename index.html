      One tap from your home screen

      No time to open the app when a pothole comes up? Set up a home-screen button that logs one the moment you tap it.

      You're in the installed app. Skip to step 5.

      Android, in Chrome. Takes about a minute.

      1. Got an old Moto Potholes icon? Hold it and tap Remove or Uninstall first. Old icons don't get the shortcut.

      2. Open this site in Chrome. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right, then Add to Home screen.

        New tabBookmarksAdd to Home screenSettings

      3. Choose Install. Not Create shortcut, which makes a plain bookmark that can't do one-tap logging.

        ✓ Install✗ Create shortcut

      4. Open Moto Potholes from your home screen. No Chrome address bar at the top means it's installed properly. Tap Allow when it asks for location.

      5. Press and hold the Moto Potholes icon. Tap Log a pothole and it's logged straight away.

        ⚠ Log a potholeApp info

      6. Want it as its own button? Hold Log a pothole in that menu and drag it onto your home screen.

      Testing it? Delete the test pothole from To send afterwards so it comes off the riders' map.

      iPhone: iPhones don't do shortcuts. In Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. The app opens with the big button at the top, ready to tap.

      Riders' pothole map

      Every pothole riders have logged. Only the spot, which way the rider was going, and how long ago are shared. Tap a pin to say it's gone, “fixed”, or still there. Once enough riders say it's gone, it comes off the map.
